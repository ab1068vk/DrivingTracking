# Product Disclaimer

## Purpose of the application

Road Sage records and summarises driving that has already occurred. It is a personal
driving-record and review tool.

## Not a driving aid

Road Sage does not intervene in driving and is not a substitute for attention, judgement or
compliance with traffic law. Do not interact with the application while driving. Alerts and
notifications are informational and must not be relied upon for driving decisions.

## No guarantee of trip detection

Automatic trip detection relies on satellite positioning and Android activity recognition, both of
which are probabilistic. Trips may start or end late, be missed, be split, or be recorded when the
device holder was not driving. Manual recording is provided for this reason.

## No guarantee of speed-limit accuracy

Speed-limit information is assembled from map data, statistical learning from observation, and
user confirmation. Coverage is incomplete, data can be outdated, and temporary, variable or
unposted limits may not be represented. Speed-limit information presented by the application must
not be relied upon to determine the lawful speed for a road.

## Speed-sign scanning

Sign recognition depends on optical conditions and sign condition and can misread or fail to read
a sign. A scan result is evidence used by the application; it is not a verified reading of the
posted limit.

## Driving scores are informational

Scores summarise recorded events using provisional thresholds and are accompanied by a confidence
level. A score is not a safety rating, an assessment of driver competence, a certification, or a
prediction of future outcomes.

## Not evidence for legal, insurance or disciplinary use

Recorded data originates from consumer-grade sensors with known uncertainty, may be incomplete,
and may expire under retention settings. The application is not designed, validated or intended to
produce evidence for legal proceedings, insurance claims, employment decisions or disciplinary
processes.

## No identification of the driver

The application records trips made with the device. It cannot determine who was driving or who was
using the phone.

## Device and platform dependence

Background capture depends on Android platform behaviour, manufacturer battery management and
granted permissions, and varies by device. Some capabilities are unavailable outside Android.

## Network-dependent features

Weather and road-data enrichment depend on third-party services and network availability. These
features may be unavailable, delayed or incomplete, and the application does not control the
accuracy of third-party data.

## Data loss

Data is stored on the device. Device loss, damage, reset or uninstallation may result in permanent
data loss unless a backup exists and has been verified by restoring it. Passphrase-protected
backups cannot be recovered if the passphrase is lost.

## Qualification status

Software qualification is complete for the current branch. **Physical-device qualification has not
been performed.** No claim is made about real-world accuracy, reliability or performance.

## Monitoring other people

Road Sage must not be used to track, score, evaluate, supervise, discipline, insure, employ,
price or monitor another person — including an employee, contractor, family member, minor, shared
device or vehicle — without every consent, disclosure, permission and legal basis required in the
user's jurisdiction. Surveillance, recording, employment, insurance and privacy laws vary by place
and change over time.

## Not for adverse decisions

Application outputs must not be used on their own to make legal, insurance, employment, lending,
pricing, disciplinary, eligibility, safety, medical, tax, fleet or compliance decisions about the
user or anyone else. Verify important decisions against primary records and qualified
professionals.

## No emergency monitoring

Road Sage does not monitor the user for emergencies, does not promise crash detection, and does
not replace roadside assistance, insurance claims processes, law enforcement, medical help or
human supervision.

## Alerts may not arrive

Voice alerts, notifications, possible-incident checks, phone-use warnings, speeding checks,
route-risk messages and maintenance reminders can be late, muted, blocked, missed, unavailable or
wrong. They must not be depended on to prevent harm.

## Vehicle maintenance information

Maintenance reminders and driving-load signals are planning aids, not diagnostics, component-life
measurements, repair instructions, or permission to delay service. Requirements vary by model
year, market, trim, powertrain, equipment, service history, recalls and operating conditions.
Where the owner or warranty manual, tyre placard, vehicle alert, recall notice or a qualified
technician conflicts with Road Sage, that source governs.
